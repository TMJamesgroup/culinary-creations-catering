Full-service pricing acknowledgment

Full-service catering includes an additional 22% service charge, calculated on the applicable catering subtotal. This charge helps cover event planning and coordination, service personnel, setup, breakdown, equipment handling, transportation logistics, and other operational expenses. This is not a gratuity or tip and is retained by Culinary Creations Catering to support these operations, rather than being paid directly to individual employees. The charge is itemized in your estimate and final proposal. Bartending, specialty rentals, and other additional services may be quoted separately.

Welcome to Culinary Creations Wholesale

A dependable catering partner for event planners, venues, hospitality businesses, caterers and organizations that need quality food and reliable service. Learn how our wholesale program works while your application is reviewed.

Food-Only Wholesale Supply

Source prepared catering food for your own events and customers. Discuss buffet favorites, BBQ, breakfast, cold catering, appetizers and specialty selections.

Pickup, delivery and recurring supply arrangements are reviewed based on your needs and service area.

Venue & Event Support

Work with us on weddings, corporate functions, private parties and venue events. Options may include food preparation, buffet setup, staffed service and event coordination.

Staffing, equipment and special event requirements are quoted for each project.

Reseller & White-Label Catering

For qualified partners, discuss supplying food that your company markets as part of its own catering or event offering. We can review packaging, pickup or delivery, volumes and operational expectations.

White-label arrangements require separate approval and agreed terms.

Private Partner Pricing

Wholesale rates are not the same as public catering menu prices. Approved accounts may receive a private price list based on the type of partnership, expected order volume and service requirements.

No pricing or discount is guaranteed by submitting an application.

How the Partnership Process Works

1. Application received. We review your business, requested services and expected event volume.

2. Follow-up. Our team may contact you to discuss menu needs, delivery area, event frequency and operating requirements.

3. Terms and pricing. Qualified businesses can review proposed services and individually approved wholesale terms.

4. Account access. Once the secure partner portal is launched, approved partners will be able to sign in to access their authorized pricing and account information.

Planning Your Wholesale Orders

Helpful details to prepare include your anticipated guest counts, approximate orders per month, preferred menus, pickup or delivery location, notice periods and any dietary or presentation requirements. These details help us develop a workable supply arrangement.

Availability, minimum quantities, lead times, delivery and payment terms will be confirmed during the approval and quotation process.
