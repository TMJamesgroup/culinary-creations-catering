Welcome to Culinary Creations Wholesale

A dependable catering partner for event planners, venues, hospitality businesses, caterers and organizations that need quality food and reliable service. Learn how our wholesale program works while your application is reviewed.

Food-Only Wholesale Supply

Source prepared catering food for your own events and customers. Discuss buffet favorites, BBQ, breakfast, cold catering, appetizers and specialty selections.

Scheduled pickup is standard; optional one-way delivery is available at standard rates.

Venue & Event Support

Work with us on weddings, corporate functions, private parties and venue events. Wholesale orders cover prepared food for pickup or optional one-way delivery. Event staffing may be arranged separately at standard rates.

No serving equipment or event setup is included.

Reseller & White-Label Catering

For qualified partners, discuss supplying food that your company markets as part of its own catering or event offering. We can review packaging, pickup or delivery schedules, volumes and operational expectations.

White-label arrangements require separate approval and agreed terms.

Private Partner Pricing

Our three programs are Wholesale Catering (20%), Wholesale Reseller (30–35%) and White-Label Catering (40–50%). Discounts apply to eligible food only, packed in disposable catering containers.

These are informational program rates, not an approved individual price agreement.

Wholesale Food Pricing

Three Programs. One Simple Food-Only Model.

Choose the partnership that fits your business. All wholesale discounts apply only to eligible prepared food, packed in disposable catering containers for scheduled pickup or optional delivery.

Wholesale Catering — 20% Off

For approved event planners, venues, businesses and occasional catering buyers. Order eligible menu items at 20% below standard food prices without a recurring purchase commitment.

Wholesale Reseller — 30–35% Off

For approved businesses regularly purchasing food to resell to their customers. Discount level depends on agreed order volume, purchasing commitments and eligible food items.

White-Label Catering — 40–50% Off

For contracted partners selling our prepared food under their own business name. Pricing is individually approved based on production cost, volume and agreed supply terms. Food is still packed in standard disposable containers.

Example: An eligible food item with a standard price of $100 costs $80 in Wholesale Catering, $65–$70 in Wholesale Reseller, or $50–$60 in White-Label Catering, depending on the partner's approved discount.

Discounts are limited to eligible food. Higher reseller and white-label rates require review and approval; premium or specialty items may have separately approved pricing. No staffing, serving equipment, setup, or rentals are included. Pickup is standard; optional delivery is charged at standard rates.

What's Included With Your Food Order

Included: Prepared food in disposable catering containers.

Pickup: Scheduled pickup is the standard option, with no delivery charge.

Delivery: Optional transportation and drop-off at standard catering delivery rates. Delivery does not include setup, serving or equipment.

Staffing and equipment: Not included with food; available as separately priced optional services.

Standard Wholesale Service Fee — 15%

A 15% service fee applies to every wholesale food order across Wholesale Catering, Wholesale Reseller and White-Label Catering. The fee is calculated after the approved food discount, using the discounted food subtotal. It is itemized separately on the quote.

Example: $1,000 eligible food order − 20% wholesale discount ($200) = $800 discounted food subtotal. Standard 15% wholesale service fee = $120. Total: $920 before any separately requested delivery, staffing, equipment or transport-container charges.

Wholesale food orders use this 15% service fee instead of the standard 22% full-service catering fee; the two fees are not stacked. Staffing, if separately arranged, is priced at standard rates. Pickup is standard; optional delivery is charged at standard rates.

How the Partnership Process Works

1. Application received. We review your business, requested services and expected event volume.

2. Follow-up. Our team may contact you to discuss menu needs, delivery or pickup arrangements, event frequency and operating requirements.

3. Terms and pricing. Qualified businesses can review proposed services and individually approved wholesale terms.

4. Account access. Once the secure partner portal is launched, approved partners will be able to sign in to access their authorized pricing and account information.
